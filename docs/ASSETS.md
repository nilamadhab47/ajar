# Assets and licensing

## Origin

Every bitmap in `public/assets` is **original work generated by code in this repository**. No third-party art
packs, fonts, sprites or images are included, and nothing is loaded from external URLs. Nothing was copied or
traced from Gather or any other product. You own these assets along with the rest of the code.

| Output | Generated from | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `public/assets/tiles/office-tiles.png` | `src/art/tiles.ts` | 32 px tiles, 1 px margin, 2 px spacing (extruded to avoid seams) |
| `public/assets/furniture/furniture.png` + `.json` | `src/art/furniture.ts` | 84 frames, Phaser JSON-hash atlas |
| `public/assets/characters/sample-*.png` | `src/features/avatar/render/compose-avatar.ts` | Reference sheets only; avatars are composed at runtime per appearance |
| `public/maps/*.json` | `scripts/build-maps.ts` | Tiled-compatible JSON |

Regenerate with `npm run assets` and `npm run maps`. Output is deterministic (seeded RNG).
`npx tsx scripts/preview.ts public/maps/demo-office.json <outDir>` flattens a map and sample avatars into PNGs
for review.

## Status: starter art

All current art is **temporary starter art**: cohesive, usable for demos, and meant to be replaced or extended by
an artist. To replace it without touching engine code:

- **Furniture**: keep the frame names in `furniture.json` (they are the `sprite` values in
  `object-catalog.ts`) and supply a new atlas. Sprites are bottom-aligned to their footprint and may be taller
  than it.
- **Tiles**: keep the tile order of `TILE_NAMES` in `src/art/tiles.ts`, or regenerate the maps.
- **Characters**: either extend the drawers in `compose-avatar.ts`, or replace `composeAvatarSheet` with a
  compositor that stacks hand-drawn layer PNGs. The sheet layout is fixed in `avatar/data/animations.ts`
  (8 columns × 6 rows of 32 × 48 px; rows: down, right, up, left, actions, celebrate).

## Purchased pack (used by the landing page only)

`Office_Pixel_Art_Premium_v1.0/` is Office Pixel Art Premium v1.0 by Imersa Studios (32×32, top-down 3/4).
The license allows use inside this app, including edits. It forbids reselling or redistributing the files
themselves, and forbids AI training. The folder is gitignored. Credit, if shown: "Office Pixel Art by Imersa Studios".

It contains 12 floor tiles, modular walls, 77 props with anchors and footprints, 7 Tiled rooms, and 5 characters
(idle and walk, four directions). Character frames are 32×32 with feet at pixel (16, 30). Ajar's people are a
layered 32×48 sheet, so these five characters do not replace the avatar builder. Floors, walls, and furniture
can replace the generated atlases later without changing the engine.

The bitmaps in `public/assets` are still the generated starter art.
